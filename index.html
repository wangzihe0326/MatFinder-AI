About MatFinder AI

A focused workflow for early material selection

MatFinder AI turns product requirements into ranked material candidates, explains the match, and produces a report you can review with engineering, sourcing, or suppliers.

Problem

Early material selection is slow because requirements are written in natural language while material data lives in tables, datasheets, and supplier notes.

Solution

MatFinder AI turns a plain-language requirement into a ranked shortlist, explains the match, supports comparison, and exports a selection brief.

Dataset

0 materials

0 categories

Recommendation Logic

The engine parses Chinese and English requirements, detects performance needs, scores each material, and explains matched reasons and limitations.

  1. Parse the requirement for material, performance, application, and constraint signals.
  2. Match those signals against tags, properties, uses, and numeric thresholds in the local dataset.
  3. Rank candidates with explainable reasons, warnings, and similar alternatives.

Key features

  • Requirement-to-shortlist recommendation workflow.
  • Searchable, filterable material catalog.
  • Side-by-side comparison for up to three materials.
  • PDF-style report export for selection reviews.

Current Limitations

Scores summarize how well each material matches the detected requirements, with reasons and warnings shown beside every recommendation.

Before final selection

  • Confirm grade-level datasheets and supplier availability.
  • Validate processing method, environment, and long-term load.
  • Use the exported report as a selection brief, not a final certification.

Future Roadmap

  • Grade-level datasheet links and supplier records.
  • More domain presets for automotive, electronics, medical, packaging, and sustainability use cases.
  • Richer export templates for engineering reviews and sourcing conversations.
